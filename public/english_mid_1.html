Past Simple, Past Continuous and Used To

Choose between a finished action, an action in progress, and a past habit.

EnglishMeaning / useExample
was / were + -ingAn action in progress at a past momentI was reading at 8 pm.
Past simpleA completed actionI read a book yesterday.
when + past simpleA short event interrupts a longer oneI was cooking when the phone rang.
while + past continuousTwo longer actions at the same timeShe was studying while he was sleeping.
used to + verbA past habit or state that is no longer trueI used to live by the sea.
would + verbA repeated past habit (not a state)Every summer we would visit my grandparents.
didn't use to + verbA negative past habit or stateI didn't use to like coffee.
was / were going toA plan in the past that did not happenI was going to call you, but I forgot.

Good to know

  • Do not use the continuous with state verbs: "I knew the answer", not "I was knowing".
  • Use would only for repeated actions, not states: "I used to have a bike", not "I would have a bike".
Practice: Write four sentences about your childhood: two with used to and two with was/were + -ing.
