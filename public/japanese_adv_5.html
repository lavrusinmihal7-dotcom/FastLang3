Reasons and Connecting Ideas

Connectors let you give reasons and join ideas into longer sentences.

JapaneseRomajiMeaning
からkarabecause (the reason comes first)
のでnodebecause (softer and more polite)
のにnonialthough (with a feeling of surprise)
けど / けれどもkedo / keredomobut (casual / more polite)
しshiand what is more (listing reasons)
ためにtame niin order to / because of
～ながら~nagarawhile doing (two actions at once)
疲れたので、早く寝ますtsukareta node, hayaku nemasuI am tired, so I will go to bed early
勉強したのに、わかりませんbenkyou shita noni, wakarimasenEven though I studied, I do not understand
音楽を聞きながら、勉強しますongaku o kikinagara, benkyou shimasuI study while listening to music

Good to know

  • ので sounds softer than から, so it is better for polite explanations and excuses.
  • ながら attaches to the verb stem: 食べながら, not 食べるながら.
Practice: Explain in two sentences why you are learning Japanese, using ので and ために.
